about=EXPERIMENTAL PRE-RELEASE. Pick an area by drawing a search box or using the map extent, see the 5x5 km sheet grid (EPSG:3021) on the map, choose an edition per sheet, and download and load the georeferenced scans of Lantmäteriet's open Ekonomiska kartan. Sheets whose scan is larger than the grid cell (wrongly georeferenced in the source .tfw/.TAB) are repaired on load and flagged when the position is only measured. Data is fetched anonymously from Lantmäteriet's open data FTP server.
